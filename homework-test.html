2How would you like to show Buddy?

Choose a clear photo of printed homework, then tap Scan.

Buddy can use free on-device OCR to read printed worksheet words. The scanner needs internet to download its engine. Handwriting and math symbols may scan badly: always check and correct the text. For a whole worksheet, keep just one question at a time in the homework box.

Speech input depends on your phone/browser and may use its speech service. You can always type instead.

These are suggested elementary skills, not exact textbook lesson numbers. You can type the teacher's exact assignment below.

Don't include your full name, address, school ID, or passwords. This test does not send homework to its own server. Scanning uses an OCR library downloaded from a third-party CDN; recognition runs in the browser. Voice dictation may use your browser's speech service.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Corner · How this works

This test works as a guided homework coach. It can scan printed text in photos, check simple typed arithmetic, and offer age-appropriate thinking prompts in other subjects. It does not automatically know an assignment's correct answer, reliably read handwriting, interpret diagrams, or connect to a teacher or school account. For reading, writing, science, and social studies, a parent or teacher may need to review the final answer.

No child registration, advertising, analytics, or external AI service is included. The selected grade may be saved on this device. Assignment text and photos stay in this browser session and are not sent to our server. On first scan, an external OCR library, worker, and language data are downloaded; recognition runs on the device. Voice input may use the browser's speech service and microphone permission. Nothing is posted to GitHub by using the page.

The suggested lesson categories are inspired by general Alabama elementary learning goals; they are not a reproduction of HMH, Open Court, STEMscopes, Studies Weekly, or any district-licensed material.
