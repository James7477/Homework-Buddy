👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Corner · How this works

This test works as a guided homework coach. It can check simple typed arithmetic and offer age-appropriate thinking prompts in other subjects. It does not automatically know an assignment's correct answer, interpret photos, or connect to a teacher or school account. For reading, writing, science, and social studies, a parent or teacher may need to review the final answer.

No child registration, advertising, analytics, or external AI service is included. The selected grade may be saved on this device. The assignment text and picture are only kept while this page is open. Voice input, when available, may use the browser's speech service and require microphone permission. Nothing is posted to GitHub by using the page.

The suggested lesson categories are inspired by general Alabama elementary learning goals; they are not a reproduction of HMH, Open Court, STEMscopes, Studies Weekly, or any district-licensed material.
